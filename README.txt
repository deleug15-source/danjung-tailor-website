Important about Google Calendar:
The current implementation adds the fitting event to the CUSTOMER'S Google Calendar and then asks them to confirm via WhatsApp. To book directly into the BUSINESS calendar automatically, you would need either:
1) a Google Calendar Appointment Schedule public booking URL, or
2) a deployed Google Apps Script / backend with calendar permissions.
